Mini Modeler Support

Questions, bug reports or feature ideas? Write to daishixsi@mac.com.

When reporting a problem, please include

  • Your device (for example iPad Pro 13-inch) and iPadOS / iOS version
  • The app version (Settings → Mini Modeler)
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead
  • If possible, a screenshot or the model file

Frequently asked questions

Where are my files stored?
On your device only. Mini Modeler has no cloud and no account. Files you save are available in the Files app under "On My iPad" / "On My iPhone" → Mini Modeler.

Which formats can I import and export?
STL and OBJ can be opened and saved, scenes can be exported as glTF / GLB, and renders as PNG or EXR or saved directly to Photos.

Renders take long or the app slows down on big models.
Path tracing is demanding. Lower the sample count or the render size for previews, and use an iPad Pro for very large scenes.
