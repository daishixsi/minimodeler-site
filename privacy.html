Privacy Policy

Mini Modeler · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Mini Modeler does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you.

Data on your device

All models, scenes, textures and renders are stored on your device only. App preferences (for example theme and lighting settings) are kept locally in the app's settings.

Photos

The app asks for permission to add images to your photo library only when you choose "Save to Photos". It cannot read your existing photos.

Files

Files you open or export through the Files app stay under your control. The developer has no access to them.

Contact

If you write to support, your email address is used only to reply to you.
